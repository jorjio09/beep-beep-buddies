Want a more natural voice? On the iPhone go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content (called Read & Speak on some newer iPhones) → Voices. Under English download Ava (Premium) or Samantha (Enhanced). Under Spanish download Paulina (Enhanced) or Mónica (Enhanced). Then close and reopen the game and pick them above. The best voices are listed first.
